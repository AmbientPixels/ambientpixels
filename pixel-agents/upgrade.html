Questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your Stripe dashboard anytime. You keep Pro benefits until the end of your billing period.
When do I start earning more?
Immediately. Your run weight jumps to 1.5x and revenue share to 70% as soon as your subscription is active.
Do I lose my agents if I cancel?
No. Your agents stay live (up to 5). You just go back to Free tier rates — 50% share, 1x weight, 25 runs/day.
Is there a free trial?
No trial, but you can see your estimated earnings on the analytics page before upgrading.
