v2.3

July 29, 2026

  • Pro subscriptions are now enforced at run time — Pro means genuinely unlimited daily runs
  • Run packs now work — purchased credits carry you past the free daily allowance, and a credit is only spent when your run actually succeeds
  • Free tier tuned: signed-in members get 10 runs per day (anonymous stays at 5 — signing in doubles your allowance)

  • Billing hiccups can never block your free runs — the platform always fails open to the free tier
