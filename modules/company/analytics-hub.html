What this shows. Real-time site traffic from Application Insights (10-minute cache). Page Views and Sessions reflect actual visits; Top Pages, Top Referrers, and Top Campaigns are aggregated from the page-view stream.

Trust signals (coming Phase 4b). Each Top Pages row will show a colored dot based on the session-to-view ratio: green = mostly real distinct visits, amber = mixed traffic, red = bot-like or refresh-heavy.

Why Top Campaigns is often empty. Campaigns only register when links are posted with ?utm_source=...&utm_campaign=... tags. If Echo / Scribe aren’t appending them, this panel stays blank. A diagnostic log in Application Insights records how many page views in the window contain utm_ params.

Why P95 Load matters more than P50. P50 is the median fast case; P95 is the slow tail that real users actually hit on flaky connections or first paint. Anything above 3000ms is flagged red.
