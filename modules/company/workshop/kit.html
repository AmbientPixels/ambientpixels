Rules

  • Tokens are AmbientOS v1.1 (--c-*). Colour means status only.
  • No inline styles, no <style> blocks, no el.style — classes only.
  • A page loads company.css, sidebar.css, hq-kit.css and at most one page stylesheet of 150 lines or fewer.
  • At most 7 rows per section, 4 stats per row, 4 sections above the fold. No tabs inside tabs, no UI modes.
  • Every number names its window and source. A number we can't measure says "not measured", never 0.
