Activation Mode
What is Activation Mode?
Activation Mode controls how much authority agents have to change the system. It does not bypass approvals or safety gates—those still apply.
  • manual — agents can analyze and propose changes, but cannot modify tasks or memory.
  • supervised_autonomous — normal operations: agents can update tasks under governance gates (objectives, caps, allowlists).
  • experimental — higher throughput testing: caps are increased, but governance gates still apply.
Tip: Use manual when tuning prompts or investigating issues. Use supervised_autonomous for day-to-day runs.
