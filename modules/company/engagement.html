About this page

What this shows. Every real interaction with our posts, as a row you can read — who replied, what they said, which post they were replying to, and whether anyone has answered them. Last 60 days.

Why rows and not a chart. Four months of posting produced 65 interactions. At that volume an average and a trend line both round to noise, and the only useful action is to read every one and reply. The Analytics Hub is the aggregate view; this is the conversation.

Reply text is Bluesky only. Replies are harvested via AT Protocol getPostThread, which is free and public. X and LinkedIn have no equivalent we pay for, so they contribute likes and reposts but no conversation. An empty Bluesky section means nobody replied; an absent X section means we never asked.

Segments. Needs a reply is nobody has drafted anything yet, plus the conversations whose draft task died and produced nothing. In progress is Scribe drafting or a draft waiting on your approval. Answered shipped. Skipped was declined, by you or by a guard. Drafting is CEO-gated as always — nothing here posts on its own.

Median response time. Hours between someone replying to us and our reply going out. It reads not yet below three answered conversations, because two is an anecdote, and it never shows a confident zero: conversations answered before we started stamping answeredAt are counted separately rather than scored as instant.

Deep links. engagement.html#er_<id> opens on one conversation and highlights it, so an alert can point at a person instead of at a dashboard.

Where the data comes from. engagementReplies, written by companyHeartbeat/engagement-reply.js on the daily outcomeRefresh cron. If the store has never been written the page says so rather than showing a quiet, healthy-looking zero.
