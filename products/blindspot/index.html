§ 01 — HOW IT WORKS

Build a fighter,
take ten bosses.

Three moves from a blank card to the top of the ladder.
01

Build a
card.

Five steps in Quick Build or full control in the Forge. Pick an element, tune stats, pick a stance — the card is yours to evolve.

  • Quick Build
  • 5 steps
  • Forge for depth
02

Clear the
campaign.

Ten bosses with pre-boss CYOA adventures. Earn Sparks, loot crates, cosmetics, and battle charms that change how your card fights.

  • 10 bosses
  • CYOA adventures
  • Loot
03

Async
PvP.

Queue a defense deck, attack strangers, earn passive Sparks. Nemesis tracking per card — the other player is always a real human’s build.

  • Elo ranking
  • Defense queue
  • Revenge
