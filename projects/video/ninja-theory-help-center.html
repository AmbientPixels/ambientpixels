§ 01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Motion design, narrated by Ava.

The new Ninja Theory Help Center brought to life through animation, AI narration, and SSML-tuned pacing.

This animated showcase brings the new Ninja Theory Help Center on support.Xbox.com → to life — highlighting platform features for titles like Hellblade and Senua’s Saga: Hellblade II. Developed in close collaboration with the Xbox Support content team, the video blends dynamic motion design with AI-assisted production. All visuals were crafted in Adobe After Effects, with narration by Ava — a custom Azure AI voice agent — tuned via SSML for natural clarity, tone, and pacing.

“In addition to animation, I also had the opportunity to serve as a producer on the Help Center website itself — helping shape its structure, tone, and accessibility strategy.” — Chad Martin (Pixelpusher)
