§ 01 — INTRODUCTION

A symbol from school desks,
now on-chain.

The Cool S — doodled on every notebook in the ‘90s — reborn as a community token and a generative art collection.

$COOLS is a community-driven cryptocurrency project that brings the iconic Cool S symbol from 90s school culture to the blockchain. The project combines nostalgia with modern web3 technology to create a fun and engaging community experience.

A decentralized token that pays homage to the iconic mark, $COOLS aims to build a strong community around this nostalgic symbol while exploring the possibilities of blockchain technology.
