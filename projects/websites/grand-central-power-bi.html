§ WORK — SHIPPED · MICROSOFT XBOX

Grand Central 3.0.

Built-from-scratch data platform for Xbox Support — raw exports through advanced Excel logic, merged via Power Query, visualized in Power BI dashboards. 7,490 assets tracked, weekly refresh, 30+ users.

Role: Producer — Xbox Support (Microsoft)
§ 01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Static reports out, living dashboards in.

End-to-end data pipeline replacing manual audits with self-serviceable, weekly-refreshed insights for technical and editorial users.

Grand Central 3.0 is a fully custom-built data tool I designed and implemented for Xbox Support teams. Engineered the pipeline from the ground up — starting with raw Compass and CXT exports, transforming them through advanced Excel logic, merging via Power Query, and visualizing in dynamic Power BI dashboards. Every data merge, filter, sort, and transformation was crafted to handle messy, inconsistent data across multiple platforms.

This system replaced static pivot reports and manual audit requests with a living toolset — updated weekly, self-serviceable, and tailored to both technical and editorial users. Grand Central now drives smarter planning, faster link cleanups, and richer insight into what content is active, missing, or misaligned.

Grand Central isn’t just a dashboard — it’s a source of clarity. The tool transformed how Xbox Support teams audit content, uncover link dependencies, and track error trends. Here’s what changed:

  • Content visibility at scale — teams can review published assets across IA, GUID, and Contact Us modules with no engineering help required.
  • Link intelligence unlocked — with over half a million links indexed, migrations and redesigns are guided by real data, not assumptions.
  • Cloud error awareness — surface-level dashboards now show more than counts; they flag isOffline risks early, improving UX and troubleshooting.
  • Localization clarity — 71,000+ Contact Us modules across 75 locales tracked, visualized, and no longer hidden in CSV hell.
  • Publishing surge tracking — dashboards reflect key milestones like the 4,000+ asset surge in March, giving teams historical proof and future planning support.
