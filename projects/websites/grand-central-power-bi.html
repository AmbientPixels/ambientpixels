§ 01 — THE WORK

Static reports out, living dashboards in.

End-to-end pipeline replacing manual audits with self-serviceable, weekly-refreshed insights for technical and editorial teams.
§ 01.1 — the pipeline

Engineered end to end.

Grand Central 3.0 is a fully custom-built data tool I designed and implemented for Xbox Support teams. Engineered the pipeline from the ground up — starting with raw Compass and CXT exports, transforming them through advanced Excel logic, merging via Power Query, and visualizing in dynamic Power BI dashboards.

Every data merge, filter, sort, and transformation was crafted to handle messy, inconsistent data across multiple platforms. Replaced static pivot reports and manual audit requests with a living toolset — updated weekly, self-serviceable, tailored to both technical and editorial users.

§ 01.2 — what it unlocked

Visibility, link intel, error awareness.

Teams can now review published assets across IA, GUID, and Contact Us modules with no engineering help. With over half a million links indexed, migrations and redesigns are guided by real data, not assumptions. Surface dashboards flag isOffline risks early, improving UX and troubleshooting.

71,000+ Contact Us modules across 75 locales tracked, visualized, and no longer hidden in CSV hell. Dashboards reflect milestones like the 4,000+ asset surge in March, giving teams historical proof and future planning support.
