§ 01 — THE WORK

Problem,
approach, outcome.

A flagship franchise with live-ops volatility and long-tail players — deploy velocity without quality loss.
01

Problem.

A flagship Xbox franchise spanning two titles with long-tail players and live-ops volatility. Rapid response to live issues had to coexist with the premium editorial standards expected from a Gears-branded surface — across 40+ languages, every release.

02

Approach.

Built a unified content framework with modular blocks reusable across both titles. Stood up a streamlined localization pipeline — quality gates, review cycles, regional teams. Served as liaison between game studios, localization, and platform engineers. Sprint planning via Azure DevOps; CMS via SharePoint.

03

Outcome.

28+ articles published with 100% QA coverage. Deploy time on critical updates dropped from 3 days to under 12 hours. Localization turnaround improved 60%. Still live at support.xbox.com/gears-of-war.
