§ 01 — THE WORK

Cross-platform support, at flagship cadence.

Collaboration with PMs, writers, designers, backend developers — high-visibility support for the Hellblade franchise. Forms, accessibility, image pipelines.
§ 01.1 — project overview

Production for two launches.

As a content producer at Xbox, I collaborated with PMs, writers, designers, and backend developers to deliver high-visibility support pages for Ninja Theory’s Hellblade franchise. Leveraged the internal CMS to build and structure help center pages tailored for both console and web users across a global audience.

Responsibilities extended beyond page assembly — preparing content for localization, managing secure assets, and ensuring everything met accessibility and Xbox design standards. Coordinated production cycles through Azure DevOps, worked hands-on with image prep and layout decisions, advised on QA workflows for multilingual, cross-platform experiences.

§ 01.2 — behind the scenes

Forms, accessibility, image pipelines.

Built and implemented forms used for user issue reporting and feedback submission across support pages — fully responsive, WCAG-compliant, localization-friendly, privacy-ready. Provided testing guidance, helped shape best practices around form structure, verified implementation across device types and viewports.

Managed the image asset pipeline — capturing, editing, optimizing, tagging screenshots for clarity, context, and localization readiness. Verified UI accuracy, ensured branding consistency, structured filenames and alt text to support SEO and accessibility. Work sat at the intersection of UX and production.
