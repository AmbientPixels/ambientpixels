Hellblade support.

A global help center for Ninja Theory’s Hellblade series — Senua’s Sacrifice and Senua’s Saga: Hellblade II. Accessibility-first, localized across 40+ languages.
Client · Microsoft · Ninja Theory Role · Producer Scope · Help center · Localization · QA Shipped

Main Help Center hub — quick links, recommended articles, dual-title navigation.

§ 01 — THE WORK

Problem,
approach, outcome.

How this project moved from brief to production.
01

Problem.

Hellblade is Ninja Theory’s flagship Xbox Game Studios series, with unique accessibility needs — audio-based dread mechanics, vision-intensive combat. Generic Xbox support content didn’t meet the bar. Players needed game-specific guidance, globally, at launch.

02

Approach.

Produced dedicated help center hubs for both titles simultaneously. Collaborated with PMs, writers, designers, and backend dev to structure content on the internal Xbox CMS. Managed Azure DevOps production cycles. Built and validated the issue-reporting form for WCAG 2.1 AA + 40+ locales.

03

Outcome.

Shipped alongside launch. Reduced average resolution time by 45%, cut support tickets 35%, passed 100% Xbox accessibility compliance. Streamlined CMS flow cut deployment time 30%. Still live at support.xbox.com/hellblade.
