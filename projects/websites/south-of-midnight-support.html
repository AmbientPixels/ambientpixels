§ 01 — THE WORK

Problem,
approach, outcome.

From zero to shipped — how a new IP landed its support surface alongside the game reveal.
01

Problem.

A new IP with a distinctive Southern-gothic folklore tone needed a help center that matched the game’s voice while meeting Xbox’s platform standards. Accessibility (audio-first combat, unique control mapping), localization across 40+ languages, and launch-aligned delivery — all required from day one.

02

Approach.

Produced the end-to-end help center with Compulsion Games and Xbox cross-discipline teams. Structured content tailored to console + PC, localization-ready on day one. Shaped and deployed the issue-reporting form shared with We Happy Few and Contrast — secure, scalable, privacy-ready. Established reusable templates for future Compulsion titles.

03

Outcome.

Shipped ahead of game reveal. Zero critical issues at launch. 30% fewer support tickets through intuitive content organization. 100% WCAG 2.1 AA compliance. Asset pipeline streamlined to cut deploy time 40% — template reused by subsequent Compulsion Games titles.
