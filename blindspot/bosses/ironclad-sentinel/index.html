Who they are

Every fighter who falls in the Library becomes a page. The Ironclad Sentinel does not collect bodies — he collects techniques. By the time you face him, he has read the moves of every challenger who came before you, and INT scales with every round he spends watching yours.

They say if you stand still long enough in his domain, you can hear the books whispering counters in voices that sound vaguely like your own.

The Library of Leech

Books line every surface — floor to ceiling, wall to wall. Some float in mid-air, their pages turning on their own. The ink seems to move, rearranging itself as you watch — every entry a record of a defeated fighter, every paragraph a counter to a move someone once thought was original. Training dummies are arranged in the corners, shaped like past challengers, the wounds in their stuffing matching techniques carved into the wood.

Strengths

Leech drains 15% of every hit he lands as healing. The longer he stands, the harder he is to put down — and Accumulated Wisdom means his INT scales with rounds elapsed, so his ability damage compounds while you're trying to grind him out.

Resistant to strikes (-20%), so brute-force shapes lose the most efficient damage line.

Weaknesses

Weak to STR despite the strike resistance — high STR overcomes the -20% modifier, and his low base STR (6) means he can't trade. Counter does +20% damage. Lean on counter for offense, abilities for finishing damage, and don't let the fight grind past round seven.

The Scholar archetype

Scholars treat the arena like a library. Every move you make is a citation, filed away, returned to you sharper next round. Knowledge here is not metaphor — it is the weapon.
