How Combat Works

Elements

Fire — strong vs Earth, weak vs Shadow Earth — strong vs Arcane, weak vs Fire Arcane — strong vs Shadow, weak vs Earth Shadow — strong vs Fire, weak vs Arcane Chaos — neutral to all
Elemental Passives 🔥 Fire — Ignite: all crits apply burn 🏔️ Earth — Bedrock: +5% damage reduction 🔮 Arcane — Attunement: +1 starting ability charge 👻 Shadow — Phantom: +5% dodge chance 💫 Chaos — Flux: random element each round

Strong: +25% damage. Weak: -25% damage. Applies to all attacks (not Heal).

Moves

Strike
STR-based damage. Disrupts heals. Blocked by Guard.
Guard
Blocks 60% of strike damage. Stunned by abilities.
Heal
END-based recovery. Halved by strikes. Cancelled by abilities.
Counter
Reflects strike damage back. Fails against abilities.
Ability
INT-based class power. Costs 2 charges. Stuns guards.

Matchups

Strike beats Heal & Ability Guard beats Strike Ability beats Guard & Counter Counter reflects Strike Heal beats nothing (pure recovery)

Stats: STR = strike power, AGI = speed + dodge, INT = ability power, END = heal + HP + stamina, LCK = crit chance

Watch for the amber glow on move buttons — it means a combo is ready! STREAK builds across turns; any blocked, missed, or healing turn resets the chain.

Stamina

Every move costs stamina. Guard (1) is cheapest, Ability (5) is most expensive. Stamina regenerates each round. Below 3 = exhausted (30% weaker).

Hype Climax

Crits, streaks, and big moments fill the Hype meter. When it hits 100% the crowd erupts: your next attack gets +15% damage AND both fighters surge with +8 stamina. Use the climax to keep a STREAK going past exhaustion.

Stances

Aggressive +25% dmg, take 20% more, +1 stamina cost Defensive -20% dmg, Guard 80%, Heal +30%, -1 stamina cost Balanced default, no switch cost

Switch costs 2 stamina. Bosses shift stances as HP drops.
