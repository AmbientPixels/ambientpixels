Blindspot Card Gallery

 

Cards published by other fighters. Browse for inspiration; nothing here changes your roster.

The Blindspot Card Gallery is a public showcase of fighter cards forged and published by players. Every card here was built with the in-game Forge or Quick Build wizard, then published by its creator for the community to see.

Filter by class, element, or rarity using the controls below. Cards span 13 fighter classes (Fighter, Enforcer, Berserker, Caster, Hacker, Scholar, Scout, Rogue, Pilot, Guardian, Medic, Trickster, Wildcard) and 5 elemental affinities (Fire, Earth, Arcane, Shadow, Chaos), with rarities from Common through Legendary. Browsing the gallery never changes your roster — to forge your own card, head to the Arena lobby and win a campaign fight.
