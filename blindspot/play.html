How Combat Works

Strike
STR-based damage. Disrupts heals. Blocked by Guard.
Guard
Blocks 60% of strike damage. Stunned by abilities.
Heal
END-based recovery. Halved by strikes. Cancelled by abilities.
Counter
Reflects strike damage back. Fails against abilities.
Ability
INT-based class power. Costs 2 charges. Stuns guards.

Stats: STR = strike power, AGI = speed + dodge, INT = ability power, END = heal + HP, LCK = crit chance
