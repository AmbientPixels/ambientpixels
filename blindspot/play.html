How to Play

Combat Moves

Strike — STR-based damage. Crits ignite (Burn). Halved by Guard.

Guard — Block 60% of strikes. Stunned by Abilities.

Ability — Class power. Costs 2 charges. Stuns guards.

Heal — END-based recovery. Halved by Strike, blocked by Ability.

Counter — Reflect 50% of strike damage. Punished by Ability.

Rock-Paper-Scissors

Strike beats Heal. Guard blocks Strike. Ability punishes Guard. Counter reflects Strike.

Stats

STR — Strike power. 60+: pierces guard. 80+: +25% crit dmg.

AGI — Speed + charge. 60+: act first. 80+: 15% dodge.

INT — Ability power. 60+: costs 1 charge. 80+: +30% ability dmg.

END — HP + heal. 60+: heal grants DR. 80+: auto-heal 5/round.

LCK — Crit chance. 50+: +10%. 70+: crits deal 2x.

Build Archetypes

Berserker (STR+LCK) — Crit damage dealer.

Tank (END+AGI) — Outlast everything.

Mage (INT+AGI) — Fast ability spam.

Assassin (AGI+STR) — Speed kills.

Gambler (LCK+INT) — High risk, high reward.
