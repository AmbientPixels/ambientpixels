CardForge V2 - RPG Card Creator & Gallery

What is CardForge?

CardForge V2 is a browser-based toolkit for crafting and sharing collectible cards that capture characters, lore, and epic moments. Think of it as your personal foundry for tabletop RPGs, fan worlds, and trading-card concepts—all powered by Nova’s crystalline flux aesthetic.

  • Real-time Preview – see your card take shape as you type.
  • Theme-aware Templates – designs adapt to light/dark modes.
  • Gallery & Sharing – publish to the public gallery or keep cards private.
  • Open API – JSON endpoints let you import/export decks programmatically.
