§ — HOW THE STUDIO WORKS

Five stages, one video at a time.

  1. Pick a project on the home page. Its rail on the left lists the videos; the one marked ● still has work to do.
  2. Open a video. Its stages appear under it: Script, Voice, Scenes, Review, Deliver. ✓ is done, ● is where it stands, ○ is still to come.
  3. Every page starts with a line saying what it is and what to do next. Follow those.
  4. Make runs jobs on your PC: render a draft, check the scenes, snapshot frames, make a music bed. One at a time; the log shows each step.
  5. Review is where you watch. Pause and press N for a note at that moment; approve the draft or request changes. Claude reads your notes (feedback/REVIEW.md) before the next round.

Local studio (OPEN-STUDIO.cmd) does everything. The website shows the same projects for reviewing away from the desk, while your PC's worker runs.
