🚀 Quick Start Workflow:

  1. Upload Image: Drag your tile background to the image drop zone
  2. Load Data: Upload your CSV localization file or use the default data
  3. Filter & Browse: Use filters to focus on specific locales or regions
  4. Edit & Preview: Click tiles to edit text with real-time visual feedback
  5. Export: Download your updated localization data as CSV
