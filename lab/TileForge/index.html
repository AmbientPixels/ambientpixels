Welcome to TileForge

🚀 TileForge revolutionizes Xbox content localization! Instantly preview your game tiles across 52+ languages on both Xbox dashboard and mobile platforms before launch. Simply drag & drop your tile image and CSV data to unleash powerful visual text analysis, seamless template switching, and lightning-fast real-time editing. The ultimate tool to prepare your CSV files for import into Iris, ensuring perfect localization before submission. Say goodbye to text overflow surprises and hello to flawless global launches! ✨

Quick Start Workflow:

  1. Upload Image: Drag your tile background to the image drop zone
  2. Load Data: Upload your CSV localization file or use the default data
  3. Filter & Browse: Use filters to focus on specific locales or regions
  4. Edit & Preview: Click tiles to edit text with real-time visual feedback
  5. Export: Download your updated localization data as CSV
