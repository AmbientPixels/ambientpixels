§ 03 — Ways to start

A few ways to start. Your call.

01

Your team builds it.

We write the new wording, measure where you are today and report back. Your web team or agency makes the changes.

02

We build it.

With access to your site, we make the changes too. One less thing on your team’s plate.

03

Start with one fix.

Just the Apply link and headline. A small first step to see how we work together.

Try it first, before you commit to anything. A working prototype in a test sandbox, built from your own pages: a week of posts, a newsletter draft and a sorted sample inbox. Your team marks each one yes or “no, because…”. No live accounts; nothing posts or sends.

If it helps: start with one fix, and try the agents in a sandbox alongside it. Or something else entirely: what fits how your team works?
