Eight dimensions.

Every score comes from the same eight axes. Every finding in your report cites the axis it belongs to, so you always know what part of the page a fix is aimed at.

D-01 MSG.CLR
Messaging clarity
Does the headline name a real outcome a visitor wants in under five seconds.
D-02 CTA.STR
CTA strength
Does the primary button promise a specific next step, not a vague invitation.
D-03 TRS.SIG
Trust signals
Are claims backed by proof a skeptic can verify without leaving the page.
D-04 FNL.FRC
Funnel friction
How many clicks, fields, and decisions sit between intent and checkout.
D-05 SOC.PRF
Social proof
Do testimonials, logos, and numbers carry specific weight or pad the layout.
D-06 OFR.CLR
Offer clarity
Can a visitor state price, deliverable, and terms in one sentence after reading.
D-07 CNT.FLW
Content flow
Does each section advance the argument or restart it from zero.
D-08 RSK.REV
Risk reversal
What does the page do to shrink the cost of being wrong before they click buy.
